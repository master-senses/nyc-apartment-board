Under-30-day rule. NYC bars renting a whole apartment for <30 consecutive days (Local Law 18). Book temporary stays as 30+ day monthly agreements. nyc.gov
FARE Act. If a broker represents the landlord, the tenant doesn't pay their fee, and every fee must be disclosed in writing. Several posters below are brokers or LLCs. nyc.gov
Remote-renting safety. Insist on a live video tour that starts on the street. Never pay by Zelle, wire, crypto, or gift card before seeing the place. Ask for written landlord approval on any sublet.
